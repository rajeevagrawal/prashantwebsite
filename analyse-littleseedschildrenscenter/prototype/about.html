🪄 Our Story

More than a school — a place where children become themselves

Founded in 2003 by a small group of educators and parents who believed that childhood deserved better, Little Seeds has grown from a single classroom into three thriving campuses across Alameda. For over two decades we have held one belief above all else: every child carries a spark, and our job is to fan it.

🪄 Our Mission

We grow curious, confident, and kind human beings

Our approach is rooted in the Reggio Emilia philosophy — a research-based framework that treats children as capable, creative learners who construct their own knowledge through exploration, relationships, and play. We don't deliver a curriculum to passive recipients; we co-create learning journeys alongside every child. Teachers observe, document, and respond to each child's emerging interests, weaving academic foundations into meaningful, joyful experiences.

Read Our Philosophy →
🌱
Child-Centered
Every decision we make starts with what is best for the child in front of us. We follow their lead, honor their pace, and celebrate their individual strengths.
🎨
Play As Learning
Play is not a break from learning — it is how young children learn best. Our classrooms are designed as invitations to explore, imagine, and discover.
🤝
Family Partnership
Families are children's first teachers. We build genuine partnerships through open communication, parent education nights, and a true open-door policy.
🌍
Inclusive Community
We celebrate every background, ability, and family structure. Our classrooms mirror the beautiful diversity of the world children will grow up to shape.
